About the seminar

The seminar brings together researchers studying the interplay of incentives and computation to explore how strategic behavior shapes systems that are computed, learned, and run at scale. Talks span mechanism and market design, computational economics and finance, algorithmic game theory, distributed algorithms and cryptoeconomics, as well as related questions in agentic AI and alignment.

Sessions are open to faculty, postdocs, and PhD students across Institut Polytechnique de Paris, as well as to colleagues from other institutions. Everyone is welcome; no registration is required.
